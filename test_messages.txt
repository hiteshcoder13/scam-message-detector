Scam Message Detector - test messages
=====================================
Copy one message at a time into the app and press "Check message".
"Model result" is what the current scam_model.joblib returns (scam probability in brackets).
Thresholds: below 0.30 = Safe, 0.30 to 0.70 = Suspicious, 0.70 and above = Scam


SAFE MESSAGES (expected: Safe)
------------------------------
1. Hey, can you send me the notes from today's lecture?
   Model result: Safe (0.06)

2. Mom, I'll be home late tonight, don't wait for dinner.
   Model result: Safe (0.02)

3. Meeting moved to 3 pm tomorrow, same room. Please confirm.
   Model result: Safe (0.06)

4. Hi, are we still meeting at 6 pm near the library today?
   Model result: Safe (0.03)

5. Your Amazon order #402-1187 has been shipped and will arrive on Friday.
   Model result: Safe (0.23)

6. Get 20% cashback on your next recharge. Offer valid till Sunday.
   Model result: Safe (0.27)

7. Big sale! Flat 50% off on all items this weekend only. Tap the link to see offers.
   Model result: Safe (0.22)
